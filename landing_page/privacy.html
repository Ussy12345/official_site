RankRush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RankRush collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the RankRush application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, RankRush may collect information necessary to create and operate your account, provide the service, maintain your progress and manage eligible rewards.

This may include:

  • Email address.
  • Username or display name.
  • Account and authentication information.
  • XP, coins and other in-platform balances.
  • Quiz and activity history.
  • Survey participation and reward information.
  • Withdrawal or redemption history.
  • Session and account activity information.
  • Information required to detect fraud, abuse or duplicate accounts.

The exact information collected may depend on the features you use and the technical requirements of the service.

2. How We Use Information

RankRush may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Maintain XP, coins, rankings and activity history.
  • Provide available surveys and related features.
  • Verify eligible survey completions and rewards.
  • Process eligible redemption or withdrawal requests.
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and duplicate accounts.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of RankRush.
  • Improve the performance and functionality of the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Surveys and Third-Party Survey Providers

RankRush may provide access to surveys supplied by independent third-party survey providers, including providers such as inBrain, BitLabs or other providers selected by RankRush.

When you choose to participate in a survey, the applicable survey provider may process information needed to determine survey availability, eligibility, survey matching, fraud prevention, research participation and reward verification.

The information processed by a survey provider may depend on the provider, the survey and the method used to provide the survey.

Survey providers operate independently from RankRush and may have their own privacy policies, terms and data practices. When you enter a third-party survey, information may be processed directly by that provider according to its applicable privacy policy.

4. Survey Eligibility and Profiling

Survey opportunities may be selected based on information such as location, age range, demographic information, interests, device information, survey history or other eligibility criteria.

This information may be used by survey providers to determine which research studies or surveys may be available to you.

RankRush does not guarantee that every user will receive the same surveys or qualify for any particular survey.

5. Survey Rewards and Verification

When a survey is completed, RankRush may receive information from the applicable survey provider confirming whether the completion qualifies for a reward.

This may include a completion status, transaction identifier, reward information or other information necessary to verify the qualifying event.

RankRush may use this information to credit eligible rewards, prevent duplicate rewards and investigate invalid or fraudulent activity.

6. Information Shared With Service Providers

RankRush may share or make available information to third-party service providers when necessary to operate specific features of the platform.

These services may include:

  • Survey providers.
  • Cloud database and infrastructure providers.
  • Authentication services.
  • Payment or redemption service providers where applicable.
  • Technical, security or analytics services where applicable.

Third-party service providers may process information according to their own agreements, privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Storage

RankRush uses Supabase and related infrastructure to store and manage application data.

Information may be stored on servers operated by RankRush's service providers and may be processed in countries other than your country of residence, subject to applicable law.

RankRush takes reasonable measures designed to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

8. Data Security

RankRush uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information.

However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their account credentials and should not share passwords or authentication information with other people.

9. Data Retention

RankRush may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account records, process rewards, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable agreements.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, RankRush may delete or anonymize it where reasonably possible and appropriate.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your RankRush account and associated personal information by using the available account deletion functionality or by contacting RankRush at the email address listed below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or maintain required transaction records.

11. Children's Privacy

RankRush is not intended for users who do not meet the minimum age requirement stated in the RankRush Terms of Service.

Third-party survey providers may impose additional age requirements. Users must satisfy the applicable requirements before participating in surveys.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

RankRush may contain links, integrations or access points to third-party services.

Third-party services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms. RankRush is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party services.

13. International Data Processing

Because RankRush may use third-party technology and survey providers, information may be processed or stored in countries outside the country where you live.

Where required, applicable safeguards will be used in accordance with relevant data protection requirements.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion or other applicable privacy rights.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below. RankRush may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

RankRush may update this Privacy Policy when the service, survey providers, technology, data practices or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the applicable "Last updated" date.
