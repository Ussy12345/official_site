RankRush — Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Information We Collect

RankRush collects your email address, username, and device/advertising identifiers when you register. During use, we record XP earned, activity history, reward and withdrawal history, and session timestamps to operate the ranking and reward system.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to operate the leaderboard and season system, calculate and distribute rewards, detect fraudulent or duplicate accounts, prevent abuse of the rewarded-ad system, and improve app performance. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Advertising and AdMob

RankRush displays rewarded and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob and its advertising partners. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, general location, and app usage data, and may use this data to personalize ads, subject to your choices below. This processing is governed by Google's own privacy and advertising policies.

4. Your Ad Choices and Consent

Where required by applicable law (for example in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we request your consent before showing personalized ads via a consent prompt on first launch, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy. You can reset ad personalization or opt out of interest-based ads at any time in your device's system settings (e.g. "Reset Advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android, or "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS). Declining personalized ads does not prevent you from using RankRush or earning rewards — you will still be shown ads, just not personalized ones.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud). We apply industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time via the Profile page in the app, or by contacting us below.

6. Children

RankRush is intended for users aged 16 and above and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16, and we do not knowingly serve personalized ads to any user identified as a child. If we learn a child's data has been collected, we will delete it.

7. Rewards Disclosure

Coins and XP earned in RankRush are virtual in-app points, not currency, and have no guaranteed cash value. See our Terms of Service for how coin redemption works. RankRush is not a gambling, lottery, or sweepstakes product — all activities have a fixed, known outcome and do not involve staking anything of value to play.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact Usman Shehu at privacy@rankrush.app.
