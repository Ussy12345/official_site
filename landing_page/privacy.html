RankRush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

1. Information We Collect

RankRush collects your email address, username, and device or advertising identifiers when you register. During use, we record XP earned, activity history, reward history, withdrawal history, and session timestamps to operate the ranking and reward system.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to operate the leaderboard and season system, calculate and manage rewards, detect fraudulent or duplicate accounts, prevent abuse of the rewarded-ad system, and improve app performance. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Advertising and AdMob

RankRush displays rewarded and interstitial ads served by Google AdMob and its advertising partners. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, general location information, and app usage data. This information may be used to provide and measure personalized or non-personalized advertisements depending on your choices and applicable laws.

This processing is governed by Google's privacy and advertising policies. You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services through Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Your Ad Choices and Consent

Where required by applicable law (for example in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we request your consent before showing personalized ads through a consent prompt, in line with Google's EU User Consent Policy.

You can reset ad personalization or opt out of interest-based ads at any time through your device settings. Declining personalized ads does not prevent you from using RankRush or receiving available in-app rewards. You may still see advertisements, but they may not be personalized.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Firebase (Google Cloud). We use reasonable security measures to protect your information. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through the Profile page in the app or by contacting us below.

6. Children

RankRush is intended for users aged 16 and above and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If we learn that personal data from a child has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

7. Rewards Disclosure

Coins and XP earned in RankRush are virtual in-app points, not currency, and have no guaranteed cash value.

See our Terms of Service for information about rewards and redemption.

RankRush is not a gambling, lottery, or sweepstakes product. Activities within the app do not require users to stake anything of value to participate.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact Usman Shehu at privacy@rankrush.app .
